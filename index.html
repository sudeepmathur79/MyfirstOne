In Buddhist cosmology, Akanishtha is named the highest heaven — a place beyond which there is nothing higher, and within which nothing is lesser. Its Tibetan name, འོག་མིན་, translates literally as "not below."

We took the word as an instruction, not a metaphor. Every venture we build is held against one unmoving question: does this belong above the ceiling most companies accept as fixed?

The fund exists to remove that ceiling — for the founders we back, and for the standard we hold ourselves to. There is no lower version of the work.
