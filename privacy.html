Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

This website does not constitute an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security. Nothing on this site should be construed as investment, legal, or tax advice.

Akanishtha Fund ("we," "us") operates akanishtha.fund. This page explains what information we collect when you visit this site or use the sign-up form, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

If you submit the sign-up form, we collect the email address you provide. We do not collect names, phone numbers, or any other personal detail unless you send it to us directly by email.

Our hosting and form providers automatically log standard technical information — your IP address and browser user agent — as part of operating the site and relaying form submissions. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

How we use it

We use the email address you submit to contact you about Akanishtha Fund — to follow up on your interest, share updates, or discuss a potential opportunity to work together. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies & local storage

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is a single flag in localStorage that records you've seen our cookie notice, so it isn't shown to you again. It contains no personal information and is never sent to our servers.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the sign-up form is relayed through Web3Forms, which transmits your submitted email to us and is bound by its own privacy policy. We don't use any other third-party service to process visitor data.

How long we keep it

We retain sign-up emails for as long as we might reasonably follow up with you, and delete them on request (see Contact, below).

Your rights

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw consent to be contacted at any time

To exercise any of these, email us — see below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Effective" date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
